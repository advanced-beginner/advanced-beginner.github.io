## Purpose

This guide exists to bridge the gap between "Hello World" tutorials and real-world software development. Rather than just showing *how* to do something, we explain *why* certain designs and patterns are necessary.

## Content Philosophy

### First Principles
We start from the fundamental problems each technology solves, not just surface-level usage.

### Executable Examples
All code examples are tested and runnable. You can verify concepts immediately.

### Real-world Perspective
We cover trade-offs and practical choices alongside the ideal approach.
